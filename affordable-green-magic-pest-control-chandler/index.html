Details to have ready for a quote

A short list can keep the first conversation focused:

  • Whether the property is residential or commercial
  • The rooms or areas where you have noticed activity
  • What you saw, or what signs led you to call
  • When the issue began and whether it has appeared again
  • Any pest products or other steps you have already tried

If you do not know the pest type, say so. Guessing can send the conversation in the wrong direction. A description of the location, timing, and visible signs gives the team a better starting point for discussing possible service.

Avoid choices that make comparing harder

One common mistake is choosing based on a vague description of the work. Ask which kind of service is being discussed, and make sure you understand what the quote covers before scheduling. It is also helpful to compare like with like. A quote for roach control may not describe the same work as a general pest service, so ask about the difference instead of treating the names as interchangeable.

Another misstep is applying several products while waiting without first checking whether they suit the situation. Keep a note of anything you have used and tell the technician. That information can help clarify what has already been done and what questions remain.
